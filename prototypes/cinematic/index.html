Step 1 · Analysis

What the reference does.

Read from nine distinct frames of a phone recording of the reference site (a screen filmed on a desk, so detail is soft). Six frames were TikTok end cards and twelve were duplicates. I could not see the motion itself, so pacing and what is scroll-driven versus timed are inferred from frames caught mid-transition.

1Hero on a colour fieldA saturated sky field fills the screen; a single figure stands at the right edge pulling a heavy drape. A modest serif headline sits low-left with one line of copy and one button. Most of the screen is image, not text.
2Object-led wipeThe drape from the hero sweeps across the screen and becomes the transition itself: an element inside the picture acts as the mask that clears one scene and reveals the next.
3Grain dissolveScenes change through a stippled, dithered edge rather than a fade: the sky field breaks into grain at the screen's edge as the next scene comes through.
4Editorial cut-outA painted figure holding a gilded fruit, cropped hard at the right on an empty light ground; a short two-line serif line at left. Negative space carries the composition.
5Full-bleed with small typeA dense full-bleed picture of a fruiting tree with a very small headline placed inside the image, so the picture reads first and the words second.
6Push-in between stylesAn engraved scene of builders moving a giant golden object becomes a full-colour close-up of the same scaffold: the camera pushes into the image, and the drawing style changes on the way in.
7Plate and colour risingA square mosaic plate beside a statement on a cream ground, while the next scene's colour rises from the bottom through a grainy edge.
8Macro detailA hand-scale close-up (grafting a branch) fills the screen: the story zooms from idea to craft.
9One object, one storyThe same object recurs in every scene, from tree to hand to gold to mosaic, so the visitor follows one thing growing and being made. That continuity matters more than any single effect.

Keep from the current site

  • Identity: the five colours, Cormorant and Jost, the Ivory to Travertine ground, the header with its outline "Start a project".
  • Content: hero promise, five services, selected work, process, credibility, the enquiry flow and all copy rules (no invented proof).
  • Engineering: the scroll-follow approach, reduced-motion and no-JavaScript fallbacks, the story off-switch and its checks.
  • The Mineral sculpture, demoted from protagonist to one supporting 3D element, as the brief asks.

Redesign

  • Structure: the seven stages spread over the whole home page, in three pinned acts with calm editorial sections between them.
  • Imagery: full-bleed photography, renders and textures replace the single object on an empty ground.
  • Motion: diagonal and object-led wipes, grain dissolves, push-ins and masked type replace the fade and the sticky drawing.
  • Composition: asymmetric, overlapping, full-bleed and light-dark alternation instead of stacked rectangular sections.

Step 2 · Motion system

Six transitions, one grammar.

Scroll position drives every scene change; only typography reveals play on their own (once, triggered), so text is never half-drawn while reading. One rule ties it together: text leaves before an image transition starts, and the next text arrives after it lands.

TransitionHow it worksUsed forScrollEasing
T1 Diagonal wipeThe next scene enters on a slanted clip-path edge (about 30% skew) across the full screenLight scene to light scene; a change of idea60–80vhpower2.inOut
T2 Object-led wipeAn element of the picture (a slab, a blade, a drape) sweeps across and becomes the maskThe signature cut; at most twice per page80–100vhpower3.inOut
T3 Grain dissolveA stippled threshold mask rises from one edge, noise-shaped, 2px grainLight to dark and back; a change of depth70–90vhpower1.inOut
T4 Push-in match cutAn image scales past its frame; the next scene begins at that scale and settlesMoving closer to the same subject (idea to detail)80–120vhpower1.in, then power2.out
T5 Layered panelsSlanted panels rise at different speeds, then contract or expand to full bleedOne idea becoming several facets100–140vhpower3.out in, power2.in out
T6 Plate contractionFull bleed contracts into a framed plate on the ivory groundEnding an act; handing over to calm content60–70vhpower2.inOut

Image movement

Ambient zoom from 1.12 to 1.0 across a scene; pans no wider than 4%; three parallax speeds (0.9, 1.0, 1.1) for layered panels. No rotation, no bounce.

Typography

Headlines rise line by line out of a mask (0.9–1.1 s, 0.08–0.12 s stagger); short lines by word (0.05 s). Labels fade and move 12 px. Buttons and the header never animate.

Timing

Scrub smoothing 0.8 s. A scene takes about one viewport of scroll, a transition 60–120% of one. A pinned act holds 2–3 stages and lasts 5–6.5 viewports, with the skip link kept.

Choreography

Each act runs: image settles, text in, hold, text out, transition. One transition type per hand-off, chosen by meaning (closer, sideways, deeper), never at random.

Phones

Same story, shorter acts (5.2 viewports), panels stacked, smaller zooms; diagonal wipes keep their angle, dissolves keep their grain.

Reduced motion

No pin, no scrub: each scene is a full-height still in order, every text visible. Without JavaScript, the same.

The seven stages across the page

  1. 01 OriginAct I opens on the hero: one seed-like form on stone, the promise and the CTA legible on first paint.
  2. 02 AwakeningT4 push-in, T1 diagonal wipe, T5 panels: the idea opens into facets. The positioning statement.
  3. 03 FoundationT3 grain dissolve into the dark ground: research, understanding, strategy (how we work). T6 closes Act I.
  4. 04 ExpansionAct II: the five services as five connected compositions, entered by T2, moving sideways with T1.
  5. 05 DifferentiationSelected work: each project its own material and tone, T5 panels per project. T6 closes Act II.
  6. 06 IdentityA calm, confident editorial section: credibility and the proof that exists, in one composition.
  7. 07 EcosystemAct III: the final CTA as a field that keeps opening (T4 push-out), ending on the olive enquiry band.

Step 3 · Three directions

Same story, same motion, three looks.

Tiles are generated studies of colour, material and type, not borrowed images. References are named, not reproduced.

A

Gallery

The brand as a body of sculpture in a quiet gallery: stone, plaster and gold on ivory, with one move into a dark strata room for the foundations. Prototyped.

Image direction

Sculpture studio photography: casts, stone, gold leaf, hands at work, raking daylight. Renders of the Mineral form sit among them as exhibited pieces.

3D and material

Matte stone, satin plaster, frosted mineral, one burnished gold. 3D used for two or three hero objects only.

Colour

IvoryGroundTravertineStoneGoldDetailEspressoStrata roomMuted sageMineral

Typography

Large Cormorant headlines low-left, generous negative space; Jost labels with gold numerals as stage marks.

Motion behaviour

Slow and weighted. Push-ins dominate; wipes are clean diagonals; one grain dissolve into the dark room per act.

Example transitions

Origin → Awakening: seed pushes in, Awakening cuts in on a diagonal. Awakening → Foundation: grain dissolve into strata. Foundation → Expansion: plate contraction.

B

Archive

The brand as a living archive: specimen plates, engravings, pressed forms and macro photography, catalogued and numbered, then let loose at full bleed.

Image direction

Engraving-style illustration that pushes into colour photography (as the reference does), specimen drawers, macro organic detail, paper and ink.

3D and material

Very little 3D: thin translucent forms laid on plates like specimens. Paper grain, ink, glass slides.

Colour

IvoryPaperOliveInkMuted sageSpecimensMuted clayLabelsGoldNumerals

Typography

Catalogue logic: plate numbers, small caps captions, serif titles set like specimen labels.

Motion behaviour

Plates fly from an ordered grid into full bleed; page-turn diagonals; engravings dissolve into photographs through grain.

Example transitions

Origin → Awakening: an engraved seed plate pushes in and dissolves into a colour macro. Expansion: five plates on a grid, each expanding in turn.

C

Nocturne

The brand in raking light: dark espresso chapters with lit material close-ups and gold veins, alternating with ivory pauses. The most cinematic and the most dramatic.

Image direction

Low-key photography of materials (stone, bronze, leaves, hands) lit from one side; the Mineral form with its gold veins as the recurring object.

3D and material

Translucent mineral, bronze, satin; light passing through thin edges. More 3D than A or B, still not one central object.

Colour

EspressoDark chaptersIvoryPauses, typeGoldVeins, lightDeep forestShadowMuted bronzeMetal

Typography

Ivory Cormorant on espresso, larger and sparser; labels in Travertine.

Motion behaviour

Light-slit diagonal reveals (a narrow lit band widening across the dark), slow pans, grain dissolves back to ivory between acts.

Example transitions

Origin: a slit of light opens on the seed. Foundation: the dark deepens through grain. Identity: a full ivory cut, the most confident moment.

Step 4 · Working prototype

Origin, Awakening, Foundation, in Direction A.

It tests the opening composition, the push-in match cut, the diagonal wipe, layered slanted panels, the grain dissolve into a dark scene, masked headline reveals and the plate hand-off. The transitions are the same in all three directions; only imagery and colour change. Images are placeholders from the Mineral sculpture and generated stone, labelled as such.

Decisions before Step 5

What I need from you.

  • Direction: A Gallery (recommended: closest to the current identity and the approved Mineral work), B Archive, C Nocturne, or a mix.
  • Imagery source: this style lives or dies on its pictures. AI-generated images, licensed stock, a commissioned shoot, your own photographs, or mainly 3D renders and textures. The prototype's placeholders will be replaced either way.
  • GSAP and ScrollTrigger: recommended for the full build (about 70 KB compressed, free to use). No Lenis: native scrolling stays, which keeps keyboard, anchors and accessibility intact.
  • Page order: to fit the seven stages, process moves before services (Foundation before Expansion). Copy stays as approved.
  • The current Mineral story: it becomes one supporting element in Act I and Act III instead of a long pinned sequence.
