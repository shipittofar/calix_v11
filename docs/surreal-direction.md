## Why the change

Owner feedback on the generated set: too abstract, stone-like, sometimes plant- or cactus-like, not 4K. The new brief asks for recognizable real objects in surreal, art-directed scenes (editorial photomontage, advertising-campaign quality), stronger light-dark contrast within the palette, and a ten-stage story about how a brand takes shape: Origin, Awakening, Discovery, Foundation, Character, Identity, Language, Expression, Ecosystem, Beyond.

## Chosen: The Seed (14 November 2026)

One real seed, treated as the most precious object in the world, followed through every scene like the pear in the reference. It never sprouts or grows: it is examined, set, cast, turned into a mark and carried into applications, so the story stays about building a brand.

- **Object:** an acorn recommended (unmistakable seed, strong silhouette for a mark, no olive association); alternatives a peach stone or an almond (too close to an olive pit).
- **Storyboard:** 01 seed on a plinth in a dark museum hall (hero) · 02 a crack of light opens in the shell · 03 the boulder-sized seed studied on a drafting table with calipers, lens, tracing paper · 04 the seed as the gold keystone of a stone arch (process) · 05 foundry row of seed casts in different materials (work) · 06 the seed's silhouette blind-embossed as a mark on an ivory wall (Brand) · 07 the design system on a table, the seed as paperweight (Web) · 08 oversized applications in an olive dusk landscape, the seed rolling between them (Marketing) · 09 aerial city with a seed-shaped square (SEO & GEO, Growth) · 10 a new seed on the threshold of a door of light (enquiry).
- **Still to decide:** storyboard approval, the seed species, anonymous figures for scale, and three 4K test frames (01, 04, 05) for about $0.48.
